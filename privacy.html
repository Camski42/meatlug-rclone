Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-08

Meatlug rclone is used by one person, the owner, to back up a home server to the owner's own Google Drive.

  • It only uploads the owner's backup files to the owner's own Google Drive account.
  • It does not collect, sell, or share any personal data.
  • No data is sent to anyone other than Google Drive.
  • Access can be removed at any time in the Google Account, under Security, then Third-party apps.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/Camski42/meatlug-rclone.
